# Juicero (clean pitch, pre-launch ~2015)

**One-liner:** A Wi-Fi-connected countertop juicer that makes fresh cold-pressed juice at home from single-serve packs of chopped produce.

**Problem:** Fresh cold-pressed juice is healthy but expensive and inconvenient. Bottled juice is pasteurized or sits on the shelf, and traditional home juicers are messy, slow to clean, and a hassle to shop for.

**Solution:** A press you place on your counter, plus subscription packs of produce delivered to your door. You insert a pack, press a button, and get a glass of juice in about a minute, with no chopping and no cleanup. Each pack carries a code the machine reads to check that the produce is fresh, and the machine connects to our service.

**Customer:** Affluent, health-conscious urban consumers who already spend on premium juice and wellness products.

**Business model:** A one-time hardware purchase of roughly $700 [verify], plus recurring revenue from produce packs at a few dollars each [verify]. The packs are the long-term business. The machine is a high-quality, precisely engineered press that applies very high force to extract more juice than a typical juicer [verify claim].

**Market:** Cold-pressed juice is a fast-growing category, and people are paying more for convenience in food and wellness [illustrative].

**Why now:** Connected home appliances are becoming mainstream, and consumers are showing they will pay for fresh, healthy food.

**Traction:** Strong investor interest, and early demo users report loving the taste and the experience [illustrative].
