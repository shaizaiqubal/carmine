# Khatabook (messy pitch: founder rambling in an email)

hey, so the thing I'm working on. you know how every kirana store has that notebook where they write who owes them money? that's the whole thing. shopkeepers give stuff on credit all day, write it in a khata, and then half the time the entries get lost or the customer says "I never took that" and it becomes a fight. also nobody likes asking for their own money back, it's awkward.

so we're making an app where you just tap in what someone owes, and it reminds them on SMS/WhatsApp so the shopkeeper doesn't have to. it backs everything up so if the notebook gets wet or lost, who cares. has to work in Hindi and other languages because these guys aren't going to use English accounting software, honestly many of them just got their first smartphone.

it's free. we want millions of shopkeepers using it daily first, money comes later, probably payments and loans for merchants once they trust the app, plus some paid features maybe. there's tens of millions of small businesses in India and basically all of them are on paper. the other stuff out there is billing software built for bigger shops, too complicated.

why now: phones and data are cheap enough now, and UPI and digital payments are blowing up so people are getting used to doing money stuff on their phones. early users are adding a few entries a day and they're still around after week one , which I think is a good sign.
