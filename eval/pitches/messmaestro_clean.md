# MessMaestro (clean pitch, adapted closely from the project page)

**One-liner:** An Android app that reduces food wastage in hostel messes by providing incentives for students to pre-inform the mess to prepare their meal or not.

**Problem:** Anyone who has been on a pre-paid mess system knows how much food is wasted each day because attendees skip meals. The mess is bound to prepare food for everyone, which leads to wastage.

**Solution:** Students can tell the mess not to prepare their food up to three days in advance, and they are refunded money for that meal. The refund comes as credits, which can be redeemed for extra single-serves like ice cream, or for money at the end of the month. This lets the mess owner manage how much food to prepare, reduces wastage, and keeps a tab on double loaders.

**How it works:**
- The app shows a QR code that updates each day for each user and contains an encrypted unique identifier. It is scanned at the serving station as a token to get food, so the process is paperless.
- A meal-switch option lets the student pick a date and turn off a particular meal they will miss. The mess is informed and the food is not prepared.
- The student can view the day's menu and give feedback on the food, which can be used for quality control and retention.
- The admin can see ratings and the percentage of people actually eating in the mess, to help them make better decisions.

**Customer:** Students in prepaid hostel messes, and the mess owners and hostel authorities who run them.

**Traction:** We ran the model as a 3-week beta test in our college with the cooperation of the mess and hostel authorities. Food wastage decreased from the 5th day of the trial, which gave us the proof of concept. The app was trending on the Play Store in India on 12 December 2018, a day after release, and was presented at the 7th Inter IIT Tech Meet at IIT Bombay.
