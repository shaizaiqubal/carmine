# Khatabook (clean pitch, early stage ~2018)

**One-liner:** A free mobile ledger app that replaces the paper *khata* small shopkeepers in India use to track credit (*udhaar*) given to customers.

**Problem:** Millions of small shopkeepers sell on credit and track it in notebooks. Entries get lost, disputed, or forgotten, and chasing payments is awkward and takes up time. Money that is owed often goes uncollected.

**Solution:** A simple app where a shopkeeper records what each customer owes in a few taps. The app sends payment reminders to customers by SMS or WhatsApp, keeps a backed-up record, and shows who owes how much. It works in regional languages and needs no accounting knowledge.

**Customer:** Small shopkeepers and traders across India: kirana stores, wholesalers, and local service businesses. Many are first-time smartphone users who are not comfortable with accounting software.

**Business model:** The app is free and we focus on growth first. Once merchants rely on it daily, we monetize through services built on top of the ledger, such as payments, credit access for merchants, and premium features.

**Market:** Tens of millions of small businesses in India [verify], most still on paper. The existing options are paper notebooks and heavier billing or accounting software built for larger businesses.

**Why now:** Cheap smartphones and cheap mobile data have reached small merchants. Digital payments are growing quickly, and merchants are getting used to doing things on a phone.

**Traction (early):** Early users record a few entries a day and keep using the app after the first week [illustrative].
