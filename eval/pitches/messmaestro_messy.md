# MessMaestro (messy pitch: casual hackathon-style writeup)

**Mess Maestro** 🍛 android app to stop food getting wasted in hostel messes

ok so everyone who's been on a prepaid mess knows the deal. you pay upfront, skip a meal, and the mess cooks for you anyway. multiply by a whole hostel, every day = a LOT of wasted food.

our fix: tell the mess up to 3 days ahead that you're skipping, and you get refunded for that meal. refund comes as credits, spend them on extras like ice cream, or cash out at end of month. mess owner knows how much to cook, less waste, and also can see who's double loading (taking food twice).

how it works:
- open the app, there's a QR that changes daily, scan at the counter to get your food. no paper
- "meal switch" in the side menu, pick date + meal to turn off
- can see the day's menu there too
- you can leave food feedback for the mess
- admin side: ratings + what % of people actually showed up to eat

we tested it in our college for 3 weeks, with mess + hostel authorities on board. wastage started dropping from day 5. also presented at the 7th Inter IIT Tech Meet at IIT Bombay and the judges liked it. trended on the Play Store in India on 12 Dec 2018, a day after release.
